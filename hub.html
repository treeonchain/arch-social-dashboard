Content Pillars

1 earns the right → 2 sells the benefit → 3 handles trust → 4 teaches vocabulary → 5 differentiates

Pillar 1, The Unlock

Programs can now take decentralized, trusted ownership of physical Bitcoin UTXOs and clear/settle at the base layer. What earns the right to say "prime brokerage" at all.

The UTXO-ownership problem · wrapped vs. native · a validator set, not one balance sheet

Pillar 2, Real Leverage

Most "Bitcoin credit" is over-collateralized lending, no real leverage. A margin account sees collateral and position together.

Hyperliquid-for-Bitcoin-margining · lending vs. margin explainer · benefit-forward, no invented numbers

Pillar 3, Transparent Collateral

Last cycle's blow-ups were market-structure failures, not Bitcoin failures. Credit works when collateral is verifiable.

Opaque-lending post-mortems · what "transparent" means operationally · own rails

Pillar 4, Market-Structure Literacy

The sophisticated conversation about Bitcoin is about structure. Home for the repo-rate material as supporting evidence.

TradFi-to-Bitcoin vocabulary · repo/financing-rate as mechanism · live-event reads

Pillar 5, Own Rails

Arch isn't an app on someone else's chain, its own VM, consensus, validator network.

"Ecosystem, not an app" · HoneyB as proof · lower cadence, KOL-adjacent
