Narrative and thesis

What Arch is, why now, and the content structure it drives.

Positioning. Arch is shifting its lead framing from "capital markets infrastructure for Bitcoin" to Bitcoin-native Prime Brokerage. Audiences already have a mental model for what a prime broker does, venture circles especially, where "capital markets infrastructure" has no existing hook.

Two-layer architecture. The foundational layer is the unlock: programs can take decentralized, trusted ownership of physical Bitcoin UTXOs and settle at the base layer, programmable collateral as the primitive. The application layer is the GTM story: Arch Prime, the Bitcoin-native prime brokerage built on that unlock. The two layers are never presented apart, infra credibility earns the right to make prime-brokerage claims, prime brokerage is what makes the infra matter.

Audience vs. market. The target user is the self-custodial, non-KYC Bitcoin holder, Arch's moat is giving that user institutional-grade services. The target market and credibility bar are institutional, that describes the rigor, not who's meant to actually use the product.
